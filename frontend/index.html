Phase 5: The Twist — Avoidable vs. Committed Cost

Six months later: management discontinued the lowest-volume product line entirely, expecting total overhead to fall by roughly its full allocated share. It didn't. Total overhead only dropped by a fraction of that. Why?

Not all activity cost disappears just because a product line does. Scheduling capability, changeover tooling, and machine capacity are largely committed investments that persist until management actively resizes them. Only some cost pools are genuinely avoidable in the short run when volume for one line vanishes.

1. Classify each activity pool

For each pool, decide: if the lowest-volume product line's entire volume disappeared tomorrow, would this cost genuinely go away in the short run ("Avoidable"), or does the underlying capacity/capability persist regardless ("Committed")?
